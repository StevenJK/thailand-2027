Why this trip

We haven't all been in one place for years. This is the excuse.

Northern Thailand in December is cool, dry and lit up. Chiang Mai is relaxed, cheap to eat well in, and easy to move a big group around. Then Bangkok for Christmas on the river.

  • 1
    A finish line to cheer at. The marathon starts before dawn. Everyone else gets a lie-in, then meets the runners at the finish for breakfast.
  • 2
    Our own bungalows, one resort. Each family gets its own villa or room. Pick a comfortable room or a pool villa, and we're still at the same pool.
  • 3
    Something for every age. Cooking classes, an ethical elephant sanctuary, temples on the mountain, night markets, spa days, and Chiang Mai's craft cocktail bars for the uni crowd.
  • 4
    Christmas together in Bangkok. A riverside hotel, a Christmas Eve dinner cruise and a long family lunch on the 25th.

Draft itinerary

Ten nights: seven in Chiang Mai, three in Bangkok

Built around a Sunday 19 December race. Every day after race day is optional. Do as much or as little as you like.

Thu 16Day 1

Arrive Chiang Mai Chiang Mai

Fly into Chiang Mai (CNX), usually via Bangkok, Singapore, Tokyo or Seoul. Check in, swim, then a welcome khantoke dinner with the whole family.

Fri 17Day 2

Old City and Doi Suthep Chiang Mai

Temples inside the old walls in the morning, then the gold temple on Doi Suthep mountain at sunset. Easy shake-out run for the runners.

Sat 18Day 3

Choose your day Chiang Mai

Thai cooking class at a farm, or a day at Elephant Nature Park, an ethical elephant sanctuary. Runners collect race bibs and have an early night.

Sun 19Day 4

Race day Chiang Mai

Marathon starts at 3am from Tha Phae Gate. Family cheer squad at the finish, then a big breakfast, massages all round, and a celebration dinner.

Mon 20Day 5

Pool and spa day Chiang Mai

Recovery day at the resort. Nothing planned on purpose.

Tue 21Day 6

Big day trip Chiang Mai

Doi Inthanon, Thailand's highest mountain, or the White Temple at Chiang Rai. Private vans for the group.

Wed 22Day 7

Markets and Nimman Chiang Mai

Shopping, coffee, the Night Bazaar. Last night in the north.

Thu 23Day 8

Fly to Bangkok Bangkok

About 70 minutes on a domestic flight. Check in on the river, longtail boat through the canals at dusk.

Fri 24Day 9

Grand Palace and Christmas Eve Bangkok

Grand Palace, Wat Pho and Wat Arun in the cooler morning. Christmas Eve dinner cruise on the Chao Phraya.

Sat 25Day 10

Christmas Day Bangkok

Long family lunch, pool time, then street food in Chinatown at night.

Sun 26Day 11

Fly home, or keep going Bangkok

Head home, or add a few beach days in Hua Hin, Krabi or Phuket before New Year.

Dates aren't locked yet. The race is usually on a Sunday in mid to late December (it was 21 December in 2025), and the organisers haven't announced 2027 yet. If it lands on Sunday 12 December, the whole plan shifts a week earlier and Bangkok moves before Christmas. Please tell us in the survey how flexible you are.
